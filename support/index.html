Support

How to contact Yberium

Use the contact that matches the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card details or personal staff records by email.

Product and website support

For problems with the website, demo links or Yberium product surfaces, email support@yberium.com.

Controlled access

For a design-partner enquiry, email pilot@yberium.com or use the interest form. Access is not guaranteed.

Privacy

For privacy questions or data-rights requests, email privacy@yberium.com and review the Privacy Policy.

General enquiries

For other professional enquiries, email hello@yberium.com.
