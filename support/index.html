How to contact Yberium.

Use the contact that matches the issue. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card details or personal staff records by email.

01

For problems with Yberium product or website surfaces, email support@yberium.com.

02

Controlled access

03

Privacy

For privacy questions or data-rights requests, email privacy@yberium.com.

04

General enquiries

For other professional enquiries, email hello@yberium.com.
